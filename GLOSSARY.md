# Speaking Practice

Speaking practice helps a speaker prepare, record a response, receive feedback, and practice again with an improvement goal.

## Language

**Speaking mode**:
The kind of practice a speaker chooses: Impromptu, Interview, or Rehearse.

**Impromptu**:
Practice explaining a randomly generated scientific, non-political topic, with time to research before speaking.

**Interview**:
Practice answering common interview or behavioral questions, such as describing a weakness or a past project.

**Rehearse**:
Practice delivering a prepared speech against the speaker's supplied main points and audience background.

**Prompt**:
The topic or question a speaker responds to during practice.

**Preparation limit**:
The amount of preparation time a speaker chooses before recording an attempt.

**Attempt**:
One recorded audio response during speaking practice.

**Completed practice**:
A recorded attempt with completed feedback, including a successful retry of the same prompt. Analyzing the same recording again does not create another completed practice.

**Retry analysis**:
Another effort to obtain feedback for an existing recording after analysis fails, without recording a new attempt.

**Improvement goal**:
The single main focus for the speaker's next attempt, recommended by the coach or selected by the speaker from the feedback.

**Difficulty**:
The speaker's chosen level of scientific topic complexity: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

**Target audience**:
The intended listeners whose background sets expectations for the speaker's explanation.

**Retry**:
A new attempt at the same prompt or prepared speech.

**Comparison**:
Feedback describing what changed between attempts and whether the speaker improved.

**Content score**:
An assessment out of 100 of what the speaker says, using criteria appropriate to the speaking mode, including scientific accuracy for Impromptu explanations.

**Delivery score**:
An assessment out of 100 of how the speaker says it, using criteria shared across speaking modes.

**Vocal expression**:
The speaker's audible use of intonation, emphasis, and variation to communicate meaning.

**Overall score**:
An assessment out of 100 calculated as the equally weighted average of the Content and Delivery scores.

**Speech statistics**:
Measurements of an attempt, such as speaking pace and filler-word frequency, distinct from judgment-based scores.

**Saved recording**:
An attempt's audio retained for later playback alongside its feedback.
_Avoid_: Saved voice
